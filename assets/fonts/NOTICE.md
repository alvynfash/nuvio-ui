# JetBrains Sans: not licensed for redistribution

These three files (Regular 400, SemiBold 600, Bold 700) are copies of the
JetBrains Sans font as bundled in the Nuvio desktop app.

- Font metadata: "Copyright © 2020 by Philipp Nurullin. All rights reserved."
  No licence field is present.
- JetBrains has not released JetBrains Sans under an open licence, and lists it
  as a trademark on its brand-assets page.

They are included by the repository owner's choice, at their own risk, for
private use. **Do not publish this package to pub.dev or otherwise redistribute
it while these files are present.** To make the package publishable, delete
this folder, remove the `fonts:` block from `pubspec.yaml`, and switch
`NuvioFonts.defaultFamily` to an openly licensed font (Inter is the closest
match).
